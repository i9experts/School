Rules for every post

Voice

  • Warm, confident and respectful. Write as a principal speaks to parents, never as an advert.
  • Use English for the main text, with Urdu for key lines and notices.
  • Open with Assalamu alaikum in messages to parents. Use "Mabrook" for congratulations.
  • Never compare Adabury with other schools by name.

Photos of students

  • Get written parental consent at admission for using photos, with an opt-out list kept at the front office.
  • Girls appear only in full uniform with the school hijab.
  • Never post a child's full name with their photo except for achievements, and then only with consent.
  • Replace the AI sample photos with real school photography before launch.

Design

  • Use only these templates, the official colours and the four official fonts.
  • The crest or the logo appears on every image.
  • Text covers no more than about a fifth of a photo.
